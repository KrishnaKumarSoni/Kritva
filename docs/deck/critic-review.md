## Top 3 Fixes

### Fix 1: Remove every render break (applied)
Google Slides grows empty table cells to 18 pt and wraps narrow boxes differently. Give every empty cell an explicit size, size step boxes on slide 4 from their text, re-flow the slide 2 cards, and move the cover subtitle below the 2-line title.

### Fix 2: Make slide 2 scannable in 5 seconds (applied)
Drop the per-card "LOAD, ILLUSTRATIVE" micro-label (say it once in the exhibit label), spell out the B/C/D legend, and size use-case rows from their real line count so nothing overlaps.

### Fix 3: Close the argument with an ask (needs your call; not applied)
Slide 4 ends on "Code decides. The model words." but never states the next step (for example: a pilot inside Equal, scope, date, success metric). Add one line to slide 4's so-what or a closing slide. Related: the "2 vs 0 invalid agreements" proof is 7 scenarios × 1 run; more runs would make it hold up under questions.

## One Thing That Works
Slide 3's exhibit pair: a global top-down tree next to an India bottom-up waterfall with a SOM close-up on its own scale. It shows the method, the money and the segment priority in one view. Protect it.
